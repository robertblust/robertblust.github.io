    How to read it

    Five kinds, and the kind says what an entry is. Role, Project, Community, Education and Independent are the model’s experience kinds, as the model defines them: a role is a position, a project is a delivery inside a role or beside one, community is work done in public: a talk, a seat, a working group, a published case.

    The order is the beginning, the indent is the during. A talk is a day, a board seat is years, and both are community work because the audience makes them so, not the duration. What an entry under a role or an independent period says is only that it began while it ran; who commissioned it is in its card.

    A card is the file, not an excerpt of it. Kind, start, end, organization and skills read as the file writes them; the achievements are its list; the link at the foot goes to it, at the commit this page shows. A date wrong here is wrong there, and is corrected there.
