This site collectsnothinguntil you ask the chat.

No cookies, no analytics, no third-party requests. The list of what leaves your browser is short enough to print here.

One exception, and it is not ours to remove: GitHub serves these pages and, like any host, sees the request that fetches them.

What this site does

Everything this page needs comes from blust.ch, fonts included. Nothing is fetched from anywhere else, so no third party learns that you were here.

Not used at all

  • CookiesNone, of any kind. There is no banner because there is nothing to consent to.
  • AnalyticsNothing counts you, and nobody is told that you were here.
  • Third-party requestsFonts, images and styles all come from this origin. No CDN sees your address. The one exception is the chat, once you press send; where your message goes is drawn under Where your data goes.
  • Forms and accountsNothing to fill in, nothing to sign up for, nothing to store.
  • FingerprintingNo attempt is made to recognize you across visits. The language key is not an identifier.

Where your data goes

The chat at the foot of each page sends nothing until you press send, and a message it refuses before asking the model, because the day's share is spent, an address has sent too many in an hour or the chat is switched off, is never sent on. What it answers comes from the model and names the entries it rests on, and since a language model writes it, it can be wrong. Everything else, what is kept, who receives it and for how long, is drawn below from the model.

What else happens here

Nothing. This site sells nothing, measures nothing and runs nothing in the background — it shows talks and what I do.

The talks are files. The decks and the PDFs sit on this domain and are served from here. No video embed, no player phoning somewhere else.

There is no form and no account. Nothing to fill in, nothing to sign up for — so nothing that could be stored.

If you write to me, you write to me. Contact runs through the links in the footer, at the providers named there. This site itself receives nothing.

Who runs this

This site belongs to Robert Blust — software engineer and architect. It is not a company and it sells nothing.

There is no imprint yet, and that is deliberate rather than an omission: nothing here can be bought, so there is no transaction for one to belong to.
