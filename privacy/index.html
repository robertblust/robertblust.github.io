This site collectsnothing.

No cookies, no analytics, no third-party requests. The list of what leaves your browser is short enough to print here.

One exception, and it is not ours to remove: GitHub serves these pages and, like any host, sees the request that fetches them.

What this site does

Everything this page needs comes from blust.ch, fonts included. Nothing is fetched from anywhere else, so no third party learns that you were here.

stage-card, stage-card-modal = a width in pixels
timeline-kinds = each kind of experience, shown or hidden

sessionStorage
chat = the conversation in this tab
chat-size = the panel's size in pixels

That is everything that gets stored: your choice of language in two letters, your choice of appearance in one word, how wide you left the details panel on the model page, and which kinds of experience you show on the timeline page. The width is kept only if you have moved the divider, once for the page and once for the expanded view; the kinds are the model’s five, each switched on or off, so the choice holds between visits. Two numbers, two letters, one word and five switches. The chat adds two more, and only while this tab is open: what you asked and what you were told, so that following a link does not lose the conversation, and how big you made the panel. Both go when the tab closes. None of it is sent anywhere by itself, and none of it identifies anybody.

Not used at all

  • CookiesNone, of any kind. There is no banner because there is nothing to consent to.
  • AnalyticsNothing counts you, and nobody is told that you were here.
  • Third-party requestsFonts, images and styles all come from this origin. No CDN sees your address. The one exception is the chat, and only once you have opened it and pressed send: that message goes to chat.blust.ch and from there to Anthropic.
  • Forms and accountsNothing to fill in, nothing to sign up for, nothing to store.
  • FingerprintingNo attempt is made to recognize you across visits. The language key is not an identifier.

The chat

The button at the foot of each page opens a chat with the model. Nothing happens until you open it and press send. Then your message, and the messages before it in that conversation, go to chat.blust.ch, a service of this site, which asks the model at mcp.blust.ch through its tools and asks Claude, Anthropic's language model, through Anthropic's API in the United States, to write the answer from what the tools said. Anthropic keeps what it receives for up to thirty days under its own terms. The service counts what an answer cost, and it keeps the question you sent, and whether the model could answer it, for ninety days, so that I see what the model cannot yet say; it keeps no address with it and nothing of the answer. A weekly summary I read is made from those questions and outlives none of them. Your browser keeps the conversation, and how big you have made the panel, in this tab alone, so both survive following a link, and closing the tab ends them.

What the chat answers comes from the model and names the entries it rests on; it is written by a language model and can be wrong. The service refuses a message before asking the model when the day's share of answers is spent, when an address sends too many in an hour, or when the chat is switched off, and a refused message costs nothing and is never sent to the model.

What else happens here

Nothing. This site sells nothing, measures nothing and runs nothing in the background — it shows talks and what I do.

The talks are files. The decks and the PDFs sit on this domain and are served from here. No video embed, no player phoning somewhere else.

There is no form and no account. Nothing to fill in, nothing to sign up for — so nothing that could be stored.

If you write to me, you write to me. Contact runs through the links in the footer, at the providers named there. This site itself receives nothing.
